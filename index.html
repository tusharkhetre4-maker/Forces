Our Vision

To create sound technical manpower with global competitiveness, morality and social sense.

Our Mission

  • To provide the state-of-the-art technical infrastructure and motivate student developers to build modern engineering systems.
  • To provide an embellished academic and congenial environment for complete peer-to-peer technical learning.
  • To promote intellectual, moral and social democracy to ensure all round development of the students.
